About Me

I am a researcher in computational and applied mathematics with interests in scientific computing, transport and diffusion processes, finite element modeling, and quantitative data analysis. I enjoy translating mathematical ideas into clean, reproducible computational workflows that generate interpretable scientific insight.

What I Bring

My work combines PDE modeling, asymptotic reasoning, simulation, optimization, visualization, and machine learning. I care about mathematical clarity, code quality, and presenting results in a form that is useful to researchers, collaborators, and technical teams.

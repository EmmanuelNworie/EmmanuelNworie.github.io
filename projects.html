How I Build Public Repositories

I aim for repositories that are easy to understand and easy to run. That means a clear README, organized files, dependency information, comments where they matter, and plots or outputs that tell the story of the project without unnecessary clutter.

  • Clear problem statement and motivation
  • Reproducible instructions
  • Clean notebooks or scripts
  • Interpretable figures and outputs
  • Technical writing that is concise and useful
